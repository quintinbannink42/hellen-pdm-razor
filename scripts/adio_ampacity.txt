How the 4.53 mm number is measured. Filled ADIO2 copper, tracks, and
pads are rasterized at 0.05 mm on F.Cu and on B.Cu. Via drills are
punched out. At each cell the local width is twice the distance to the
edge of that layer's copper; F and B add only on the same XY. The series
neck is the highest width W such that pins 2, 9, and 15 still connect
to U12 OUT pads 8–10 and 12–14 through cells of width ≥ W, ignoring the
pads themselves and 0.8 mm around them (the pad fillet is not the neck).
That W is 4.53 mm. A 0.15 mm trace of the same pour reads about 5.3–5.7 mm
along the haul and bottoms at the same class of cut. Eight 0.60/0.30 vias
sit by the U12 OUT pads, in copper wider than this cut, to reach the
F-only SMD pads from the B.Cu haul.

Parallel slots versus that neck. The pad-only audit summed every door
around one pin and got at most about 5.66 mm of separate 0.52–0.60 mm
slots. Those slots are not in series and are not the rating. Pins 2 and
9 are the same net as pin 15, so the west doors merge into one corridor;
4.53 mm is the minimum of that corridor. The east 0.60 mm doors of
ADIO4, ADIO6, and ADIO8 stay empty: a pour there would be a skinny
finger, and the continuous path to the driver is still ~0.80 mm.

Assignment. The west chain can serve only one net. With the odd pours
and PWR_OUT copper as obstacles, giving pins 9 then 2 to ADIO2 clears
4.53 mm. Giving them to ADIO4 instead was 3.20 mm once those pours
count (the pad-only audit's 3.66 mm does not survive), which misses
3.47 mm, so the chain stays on ADIO2. Pin 25 raises the ADIO8 cage
locally and still leaves the path to U18 at ~0.80 mm, pinch about
(94.2, 56.2), in the resistor row beside the driver. PWR_OUT4's west
leg is the 16.72 mm neck and was not trimmed to open that door.
ADIO6's neighbors are SENSOR_5V, ADIO5, and ADIO7; none of the three
NC pins opens it. ADIO4 without the west chain, ADIO6, and ADIO8 were
not poured.

What is still short of 3.47 mm, and what was not done about it. ADIO4,
ADIO6, and ADIO8. No loom pin was renamed, no hole was moved, the
continuous rating in this file was not derated, and the stackup was
not changed to 2 oz.
