Standard
--------
The board file sets only (thickness 1.6). There is no copper-weight stackup,
so this audit uses external 1 oz (35 µm), the usual 2-layer fab default.
IPC-2221A external:

    I = 0.048 * dT^0.44 * A^0.725
    A in mil², 1 oz = 1.378 mil, k = 0.048 (outer layer)

Pass line is dT ≤ 20 °C, the top of the requested 10–20 °C band.
10 °C widths are listed and are not met on the channel copper.

    Current     10 °C width     20 °C width
    8 A         5.29 mm         3.47 mm
    25 A        25.46 mm        16.72 mm
    80 A        (peak)          83.2 mm
    150 A       (F1 class)      198 mm

The 8–12 mm hypothesis for 25 A is what 2 oz would need at 20 °C (8.4 mm).
This project does not specify 2 oz, so that number is not the pass line.
Solder on the F.Mask openings is the peak strategy already drawn on PWR_OUT.
It is not credited toward the continuous rating: a 0.20 mm neck is still
~1 A at 20 °C even if the opening is wet with solder.

Via barrels are not in the file. A 0.30 mm drill with 25 µm plating
(typical minimum) is π*0.30*(0.025/0.035) ≈ 0.67 mm of 1 oz copper, ~2.4 A
at 20 °C. At a full 35 µm barrel it is ~0.94 mm, ~3.1 A. One via does not
carry 8 A or 25 A. The B.Cu hops were not stuffed with extra vias: the
module-wall gaps that forced the hops are already at clearance.

GND return (J3)
---------------
B.Cu GND pour is ~108 mm wide at y=84–90 (the J3 bobbin). It narrows to
13.6 mm total at y=66 (widest single run 3.2 mm, x=82.5–85.3) where the
M1000 keepout and the VBAT pours cut it up. That is a return neck for
anything like fuse current. It was not reshaped: filling it back in means
moving the keepout or the power pours. No SENSOR_GND pour was added.

Stopped here
------------
HP and ADIO continuous ratings are not met on the copper that exists, and
the free gap beside each long run is a few tenths of a millimetre, not
16 mm or 3.5 mm. Pushing those tracks would be a new floorplan (move J3,
the SuperSeal fanout, or the module wall). Coordinates above are the necks.
Unconnected count was left at 46.
