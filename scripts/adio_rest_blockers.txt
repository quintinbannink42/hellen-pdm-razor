Serious attempt to replicate the ADIO6 pattern (wall jog → B.Cu spine → via on
driver F.Cu → B.Cu pour keepout → GND stitch of the cut-off island) for
ADIO1,2,3,4,5,7,8. **No landing was kept**: every candidate that closed an ADIO
ratsnest also opened at least one GND (and sometimes VBAT) island, so the
unconnected count did not drop. Stopped per “do not thrash.”
